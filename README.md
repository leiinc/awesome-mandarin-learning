# Awesome Mandarin Learning

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A maintained directory of free, browser-based tools for learning Mandarin Chinese.

The collection covers pronunciation, pinyin, stroke order, HSK vocabulary, graded
reading, printable practice sheets, and open word-list data. It is organized by
interface language so learners can start in the language they already understand.

> **Disclosure:** This is a first-party directory maintained by Lei Inc. and the
> NihaoLearn project. Every site listed here is operated by the same maintainer.
> No paid placements are accepted.

## Language editions

All editions are free to use in a browser and require no installation.

| Interface | Site | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | [NihaoLearn](https://nihaolearn.com) | Pronunciation, pinyin, characters, HSK, and graded reading |
| Japanese | [中国語ラボ](https://chugokulab.com) | Japanese-language explanations for Mandarin study |
| Indonesian | [Ayo Mandarin](https://ayomandarin.com) | Mandarin learning resources for Indonesian speakers |

## Pronunciation and tones

- [NihaoLearn Tone Trainer](https://nihaolearn.com/pinyin/tone-trainer) — Record a
  syllable, word, or sentence and compare your pitch curve with a reference.
- [NihaoLearn Shadowing](https://nihaolearn.com/pinyin/shadowing) — Listen, repeat,
  and get feedback on pronunciation timing and contour.

## Characters and handwriting

- [NihaoLearn Character Dictionary](https://nihaolearn.com/hanzi) — Search Chinese
  characters, review stroke order, readings, and related vocabulary.
- [Printable Worksheet Maker](https://nihaolearn.com/worksheet) — Create A4
  handwriting practice sheets with pinyin lines or 田字格.

## HSK and vocabulary

- [NihaoLearn HSK Vocabulary](https://nihaolearn.com/hsk) — Browse HSK 3.0 and HSK
  2.0 vocabulary by level, pinyin, meaning, and related characters.
- [HSK Version Comparison](https://nihaolearn.com/hsk/versions) — Compare the HSK
  3.0 and HSK 2.0 level systems and word lists.
- [HSK Placement Test](https://nihaolearn.com/placement) — Take a short no-signup
  assessment to estimate your starting level.

## Reading and assessment

- [NihaoLearn Graded Reading](https://nihaolearn.com/reading) — Short Mandarin
  passages grouped by difficulty, with vocabulary coverage noted for each text.
- [Learning Resources](https://nihaolearn.com/resources) — Open word lists,
  downloadable datasets, and links to external mirrors.

## Open data and teacher resources

- [HSK Vocabulary Datasets](https://nihaolearn.com/resources) — HSK 3.0 and HSK 2.0
  word lists in reusable formats.
- [Hugging Face Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/leiinc/nihao-hsk-vocabulary)
  — Public vocabulary dataset with license and field documentation.
- [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/leiinc/hsk-3-0-and-2-0-chinese-vocabulary)
  — Public mirror of the HSK 3.0 and HSK 2.0 vocabulary data.
- [AnkiWeb HSK 1 Deck](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/973641514) — A starter deck
  generated from the HSK 1 vocabulary list.

## Contributing

Corrections, broken-link reports, and suggestions for additional first-party
learning resources are welcome through GitHub issues or pull requests.

Please keep submissions:

- directly relevant to learning Mandarin Chinese;
- usable without installing untrusted software;
- clear about pricing, registration, and language support; and
- free of tracking parameters, affiliate links, and paid placements.
